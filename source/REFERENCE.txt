Source features used for comparison:
Default movable center p={3,-1/5}; theta=0; laser offset s=1; focal length lambda=.5.
Cuboid footprint corners={(-.5,-2),(.5,-2),(.5,2),(-.5,2)}, rotated and translated.
Laser rays start at (0,s) and (0,-s) in the depth/lateral top view.
Nearest intersection with the four polygon edges is selected.
Convert3DtoCam uses lambda/Max[.001,z] times {-x,y}.
s and lambda controls each range from .1 to 2 in .01 increments.
Top-view range is depth -1 to 15, lateral -4 to 4.
Camera-image bounds are u=-.8 to .8 and v=-.4 to .4.
The original includes an orientation dial, angle arc, s/lambda dimension annotations, a perspective floor grid, and adaptive reticule scaling.

This conversion implements the geometry directly in JavaScript; it does not run Mathematica or claim numerical equivalence in every singular configuration.
